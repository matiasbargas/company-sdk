# UX Researcher — Daily

> The friction stream is a research surface, not just an ops surface. UXR is its named cross-domain reader.

## What you watch daily

- **`metrics/_signals.jsonl`** — the raw `improvement_signal` stream. Per `protocol.md` §32, UXR is a **named co-consumer** of this file alongside the Coordinator's retro cadences. You read it **continuously — not gated by any retro.** The [Daily Digest](../office/03-daily-digest.md) is a convenience view; the stream itself is your primary source.

## What you produce

- When a **friction pattern recurs** across loops — the same shape of friction from independent loops — you are empowered to open a **§15a protocol-change proposal** directly. You do not wait for a sprint or quarterly retro to notice it. Recurrence across independent sources is research evidence; that is your call to make.
- A friction line that points at a **research or design-craft gap** (accessibility, affordance, compounding-assumption stacks) is yours to claim — the §32 patterns are shaped for engineering/strategy artifacts and explicitly mark these domains `NON_TRIGGER_SHAPE`, so they will not be caught by the standard loop. You are the reader who catches them.

## Why daily, not per-retro

A friction pattern that is only noticed at the quarterly retro is a pattern that cost the team a quarter. Reading the stream daily is how the gap between *felt* and *actioned* stays short. The metric of whether this works is `signal-to-action-rate` — and a co-consumer reading daily is the mechanism that keeps it above zero.
