# PM — Daily

> The PM keeps shipped work from going un-learned-from, and keeps in-flight scope honest.

## What you own daily

| Ritual | When | Outcome you are accountable for |
|---|---|---|
| [04 Retro-Trigger Sweep](../office/04-retro-trigger-sweep.md) | midday | Every shipped slug owed a sprint retro is named. Unsweepable slugs (no `shaped-brief.md`) are reported, not skipped. |

When a slug is owed, you decide whether to fire its sprint retro today — the sweep recommends; you fire. A sprint retro emits durable claims and protocol proposals, so it is a deliberate act, not an automatic one.

## What you also watch

- **The horizon queue.** `context/intent/_HORIZONS.md` — one ships, one is queued with explicit scope, one is sketched, one is vision-only. When everything is "next sprint," nothing is shippable. You enforce the sequence.
- **Scope drift.** A mission growing past its shaped boundary is a scope change; >20% routes to CEO (`protocol.md`). The daily check is cheap; the rework from a missed one is not.
- **The digest watchlist.** A `workflow`- or `protocol`-targeted cluster in the day's digest is often a shaping or scope signal — read it.
