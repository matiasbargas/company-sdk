# Idea-execution v0.2 — design sketch (NOT YET BUILT)

## Why v0.2 exists

Two findings from v0.1 forced this. **First**, the CISO blind audit proved `would_have_wrongly_killed:` passes schema while discharging sycophancy pressure into hedge-density — presence is necessary but not sufficient. **Second**, the §32 shaped-brief deferred Dialectic + anonymization to v0.2 because named-role critique is itself a sycophancy attack surface.

## What v0.2 adds over v0.1

### Addition 1: Loops as a primitive callable mid-workflow

In v0.1, loop patterns are baked-in phases. In v0.2, the spawning agent at any phase can call `loop(pattern, target, weakness_hypothesis)`; the Loop Coordinator (§32) drives it and the spawning phase resumes with the artifact. Reasoning: lifts §32 from "phase shape" to "callable verb," so deeper phases spawn loops when their own weakness hypothesis fires.

### Addition 2: Dialectic with author anonymization

v0.2 adds Role-Pair Dialectic as a callable pattern, with identity stripped from messages until synthesis completes (per 2024-2026 research on sycophancy in named-role debate). Reasoning: the fix v0.1 deferred — guardrails defend, anonymization removes the substrate.

### Addition 3: Retro hook

v0.2 final phase calls `retrospective-v0.1.js` inline on the shipped slug, emitting `intent-spec/<slug>/_retro.md`. Reasoning: embodies the Owner principle that self-improving is equal-importance to executing.

### Addition 4: CISO 5-dimension audit baked into Critique-Revise convergence

Convergence in v0.2 scores every `would_have_wrongly_killed:` line on the five CISO dimensions (specific strategy / specific critique cited / falsifiable property / contextual concreteness / severity judgment) and rejects outputs ≤5/15 before revision, looping back for a critic-only retry under a different agent. Reasoning: converts the quarterly human audit into an inline gate, closing the v0.1 invisible-failure window.

## What v0.2 does NOT add

- Tree of Thoughts (v0.4, high token cost, only on high-stakes ideation)
- Multi-run concurrency / semantic conflict detection (v0.3)
- Embedding retrieval over claims.yaml (v0.3)

## How v0.2 will be built

By running v0.1 on "Design idea-execution v0.2 with the four additions above." Dogfood. The shaped-brief v0.1 emits is the spec. Mario irreversibility flags from §32 carry over — anything flagged irreversible gets a Red-Team pass before landing.

## Open questions for the v0.2 run

1. Does the inline 5-dimension audit gate need a `would_have_wrongly_killed:` of its own — what bold critique line would the audit itself wrongly reject?
2. When a sub-loop is called mid-workflow, does the parent phase's provenance hash include the child's, or do they remain independent artifacts cross-linked by ID?
