## The market direction in one sentence

Practice leads at multi-client product consultancies buy team-sdk as the portable substrate that every client pod brings to every engagement so judgment compounds across the network instead of evaporating per client.

## What the framework reveals about this segment

Working backwards forces the press release to be written from the partner's perspective on the day they win. The headline is not "we use AI" — it is "our 14th engagement this year shipped 40% faster than our 1st because every pod inherits the full reasoning trail of the 13 before it, anonymized at the constraint layer, specialized at the domain layer." The customer FAQ writes itself: "How do you keep client IP separate?" (Layer B is tenant-scoped, Layer A is the firm's IP). "How do new hires ramp?" (they clone the substrate, not a Notion). "How do you bill?" (substrate-hours are visible, so scope creep is auditable). Working backwards reveals the segment's real job-to-be-done is not productivity — it is **portable institutional memory at network scale** while keeping client contexts hermetically separated. That is exactly Layer A / Layer B / Layer C.

## The team-sdk value proposition for this segment, via this framework

The configuration that lands hardest: Layer A holds the firm's house methodology (their Soul, their Constraints, their Halloway doctrine) — shared across every engagement, the firm's true IP. Layer B is per-client, contractually scoped, deletable on engagement close. Layer C is per-mission scratch. The four-cadence metabolism maps perfectly to billable rhythms: loop-level for daily standups, daily for client check-ins, sprint for review meetings, quarterly for the firm's cross-pod retrospective where Layer A gets ratcheted. Section 32 Red-Team becomes the pre-client-presentation gate. Section 18 context requests become how a junior pod borrows specialist judgment from a senior pod without poaching staff. The press release writes itself around "every pod is a senior pod."

## GTM motion implied

Partner-led, top-down, with a land-and-expand inside the network. Working backwards from the press release reveals the buyer is the managing partner who feels the network-effect pain — they have 40 pods doing similar work and learning nothing from each other. Sales motion is one workshop with two partners, then a pilot on one practice area (say, fintech advisory), then network rollout. Not product-led because the value is only visible at 3+ pods. Community-led works as a secondary motion through ex-consultants who carry the substrate to their next firm.

## Monetization shape

Hybrid: per-pod seat license + per-active-engagement fee, with a network-tier discount above 20 pods.

## Honest weaknesses of this alternative

The framework-segment fit is strong but two awkwardnesses exist. First, Working Backwards assumes a discrete launch moment with a press release — but consultancy adoption is gradual practice-by-practice, so the "press release day" is fictional and the framework's discipline is somewhat performative. Second, agencies are notoriously resistant to standardized substrates because billable differentiation depends on bespoke methodology — selling a "shared substrate" to people whose pricing depends on uniqueness is a structural tension the press release cannot fully resolve. The largest networks (Accenture, Deloitte Digital) already have internal versions and will not buy; the sweet spot is the 50-500 person mid-tier.
