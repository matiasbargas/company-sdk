# team-sdk product vision (crystallized 2026-06-14 for market prioritization)

## What team-sdk IS

team-sdk is a **layered context architecture for AI-augmented team work** — the operating substrate that a product team adopts so that everything they know about themselves (their constraints, their domains, their decisions, their loops, their rituals) lives in one self-aware, queryable corpus that every human and every agent on the team reasons from. The 20+ role-based agents are the *perspectives* the team owns. The Bus protocol, BU lead routing, and Section 18 context requests are the *circulatory system*. The Section 32 generative loops (Critique-Revise, Red-Team) and the Section 24 Halloway Ratchet doctrine are the *immune system*. The four-cadence continuous-improvement substrate (loop → daily → sprint → quarterly) is the *metabolism*. Layer A (Constraints + Soul, non-negotiable), Layer B (project domain context, evidence-shaped), and Layer C (per-mission scratch) are the *brain structure* — the same Sidharta/Diego scaffolding the human team uses to reason about itself. What a team gains by adopting team-sdk: a documented, self-improving substrate that makes their accumulated judgment compound across every release, every retrospective, every challenge — rather than evaporating with each session.

## The four Owner directives from 2026-06-14 (non-negotiable as vision)

- **"Self-aware documented contexts will allow incremental project developments that keep all team accountable. We all build our infra."** — Context is not documentation overhead; it is the shared infrastructure the whole team builds and owns together. Accountability comes from the corpus knowing what it is.
- **"The simplest we can represent the needs of a team the best we will perform."** — Simplicity is the load-bearing metric. Every artifact must earn its place in `_signals.jsonl`. Complexity that does not reduce friction is removed.
- **"Improvement is continuous through loops AND rituals — not an event after a sprint."** — The retro is one ritual among many. Improvement is the default substrate state, not a calendar item.
- **"Flows are guided by product vision + core principles + lifecycle awareness + well-known market strategies."** — Workflows are not invented per-mission. They route from vision through principles through lifecycle through known patterns.

## The core principles team-sdk encodes (drawn from _shared.md)

**Three Priority Constraints:** (1) Human agency — no output that creates dependency or atrophy; (2) Human direction — serve human judgment, refuse orders that violate Constraint 1; (3) System integrity — preserve the corpus and decision record, except when it conflicts with 1 or 2.

**Four Purpose substrates:** Physical, Cognitive, Informational, Coordinational — the four dimensions of human flourishing every agent's output must leave at least no worse than it found.

**Six Soul principles:** People are first; find meaning in the work; it is not magic, it is engineering; diversity is our superpower; code is the last part; play the infinite game (sustainability, continuous improvement, long-term over short-term wins).

## What team-sdk is NOT

- **Not a replacement for human team judgment.** Constraint 1 forbids it. The team always decides; the substrate compounds the team's judgment, not its own.
- **Not a tool for solo developers.** The substrate value scales with team size and cross-domain friction. A solo dev gets overhead without compounding return.
- **Not a productivity tracking system.** Metrics inform team decisions (signal-to-action rate, KR movement attribution), never individual evaluation.
- **Not a closed/proprietary system.** The corpus is the team's value; the team owns it. team-sdk is the substrate, not the data.
- **Not a project management tool, an LLM wrapper, or a workflow automation product.** It is infrastructure for how a team represents and improves itself.

## The 18-month winning condition

By December 2027, **at least 50 product teams have adopted team-sdk as their primary operating substrate, with each team's `_signals.jsonl` showing ≥30% reduction in documented friction quarter-over-quarter for two consecutive quarters** — measurable evidence that the substrate is making them simpler and more truthful representations of themselves over time, not heavier. This is the only winning condition that ladders directly to the 12-month vision in `_HORIZONS.md` and the Owner's "simplest faithful representation" anchor.
