## Honest weaknesses of this alternative

The combination is somewhat forced. Bezos's Working Backwards method was built for consumer products with mass-market press releases; AI labs do not have customers in the Bezos sense — they have peers, reviewers, and funders. The "press release" becomes "arXiv abstract," which works metaphorically but loses the customer-empathy discipline that makes the framework powerful elsewhere. Also: labs are notoriously NIH-prone; they may fork rather than adopt, leaving team-sdk with prestige but no recurring revenue. Willingness to pay is "moderate" per segment notes, which is generous — academic labs pay near-zero, and frontier labs build in-house.
