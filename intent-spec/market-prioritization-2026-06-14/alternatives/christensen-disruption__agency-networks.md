# Alternative: Christensen's Innovator's Dilemma × Agency networks / product consultancies running multiple client teams

**Framework:** Christensen's Innovator's Dilemma
**Segment:** Agency networks / product consultancies running multiple client teams
**Generated:** 2026-06-14

## The market direction in one sentence

Practice leads at mid-tier product consultancies buy team-sdk as a "shadow substrate" their pods install per-engagement to compound cross-client judgment that incumbent PSA and knowledge-management vendors structurally cannot capture.

## What the framework reveals about this segment

The incumbents here are PSA suites (Kantata, Mavenlink, Productive), client-portal tools (Notion-for-clients, Confluence spaces), and the Big-4-style internal knowledge bases. They overshoot on what partners need to bill, audit, and report to clients — timesheets, utilization dashboards, deliverable governance, watertight per-client data isolation. They massively *under*serve the practice lead's actual nightmare: every engagement ends and the hard-won pattern (how we landed the discovery, what the CTO archetype responded to, the three risks that always surface in fintech audits) walks out the door with the pod, or rots in a Notion page no one reads. Incumbents will not respond because capturing cross-client pattern memory looks like a confidentiality liability to their enterprise legal review — exactly the conditions Christensen predicts. team-sdk enters from the side, as new-market disruption, serving a job (cross-engagement judgment compounding) that the incumbent stack treats as out-of-scope by design.

## The team-sdk value proposition for this segment, via this framework

The configuration that lands: Layer A holds the firm's *house doctrine* (the consultancy's Soul, its non-negotiable discovery rituals, its quality bars) — shared across every engagement. Layer B is per-client, fully isolated, evidence-shaped — the client owns and can export it. Layer C is per-mission scratch. The Section 32 Critique-Revise and Red-Team loops run inside each pod, but the *patterns* that emerge (which Critique prompts caught real risks, which Red-Team framings shipped value) ratchet up into Layer A via Section 24 Halloway Ratchet review at the practice level. Section 18 context requests become the substrate for partner-led cross-pollination without violating client NDAs — the *shape* of judgment travels, the *content* stays isolated. Billable-time pressure makes ROI legible inside one engagement; the cross-engagement compounding is the partner's strategic moat.

## GTM motion implied

Partner-led, then community-led inside the network. Christensen's disruption playbook means avoiding the front door — do not sell to procurement or IT. Land with one practice lead at one mid-tier consultancy (50-300 people, the segment too big for hand-rolled wikis and too small for McKinsey-grade KM), prove the cross-engagement ratchet inside two pods over one quarter, then let that partner evangelize to peer practice leads in the network. Conference presence at IxDA, Config, ProductCamp — not Gartner. Open-source the substrate core; the consultancy network effect becomes the moat.

## Monetization shape

Hybrid: per-pod seat license for the substrate plus a practice-tier fee for cross-engagement ratchet features and house-doctrine governance.

## Honest weaknesses of this alternative

The combination is awkward at two seams. First, Christensen's low-end disruption framing assumes incumbents are *over*serving — but PSA vendors are not really overserving consultancies, they are serving a different job (billing/governance) entirely, which makes this closer to new-market disruption with a forced low-end gloss. Second, "the incumbent will not respond" is the load-bearing claim, and it is shaky: Notion, Linear, and Atlassian could ship a "pattern library across workspaces" feature in two quarters if they noticed, and consultancies are exactly the kind of high-visibility customer they would notice. The disruption window is real but narrower than the framework suggests.
